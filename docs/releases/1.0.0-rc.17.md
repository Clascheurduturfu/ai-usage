# AI Usage 1.0.0 RC 17

Release candidate for 1.0.0 that shows the full Gemini quota even when Antigravity is closed.

- **Gemini weekly and 5-hour limits without Antigravity open**: with Antigravity running, AI Usage reads the quota summary from Antigravity itself. With it closed, AI Usage asks Google directly, and Google's summary only covers the Claude and GPT models, so the card showed a single "Gemini models" row. Google does report the Gemini limits per model, though: AI Usage now reads them from both of Google's servers and sorts them into the weekly and 5-hour windows by their reset times. With Antigravity closed, the card now shows the same Gemini rows as Antigravity's own Usage panel, in the same order.
- **Gemini 4 Argon keeps its last value**: Argon's limit is only reported by Antigravity itself. When Antigravity is closed, the card keeps the last Argon value it saw, labelled with its age ("as of 3 h ago"), until that limit resets or a week passes. If Antigravity was never open on that device, the card says to open it once.
